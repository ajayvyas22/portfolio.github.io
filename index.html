About

Quality is a system, not an inspection step

I work on the part of manufacturing where documentation, data and the shop floor have to agree with each other. Across three plants — battery assembly in Missouri, metal parts manufacturing and machined components in Gujarat — the pattern has been consistent: the standard on paper and the practice at the line quietly drift apart, and the defect rate is where that gap shows up.

At EnerSys that meant reviewing and standardising 100+ quality control documents against ISO 9001 and IATF 16949, then walking semi-automatic and fully automated FANUC robot battery lines to validate whether the SOPs matched what operators actually did. At Express Engineering it meant tracing inefficiencies in a metal parts line and running a lean initiative to a 6.9% gain in production efficiency. At K.S. Exporters it meant catching a recurring CNC defect on mild steel elbows and closing it out through root cause analysis with the production manager.

My toolkit is FMEA, SPC, CAPA, 8D, 5 Whys and fishbone analysis on the quality side, and Minitab, Power BI, SQL and advanced Excel on the data side. I am finishing an MS in Industrial / Engineering Management at Western New England University with a 3.97 GPA, alongside a mechanical engineering degree from Government Engineering College, Bharuch.

  • Root cause, not symptom

    5 Whys, 8D, fishbone and FMEA applied with process engineers to resolve non-conformances at source.

  • Audit-ready documentation

    OPLs, QCPs, work instructions and quality records maintained to ISO 9001, IATF 16949 and VDA expectations.

  • Data behind the decision

    Minitab, Power BI, SQL and advanced Excel turned into dashboards and KPIs that operations actually use.

  • Cross-functional by default

    Work runs through production managers, quality specialists and process engineers — never in isolation.
